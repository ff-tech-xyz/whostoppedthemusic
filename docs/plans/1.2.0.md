## Goal

On Minecraft 26.3 you pick how music works from one PyreHaven-style screen (press M):

- **Vanilla** uses Minecraft's music selection with Play, Pause/Resume and Next for background music.
- **Basic** gives you one song list for the whole game with play, stop, previous, next and shuffle.
- **Custom** lets you choose the songs and weights for every biome and situation.

Your own MP3 and OGG files, and music discs, can be used in Basic and Custom. Music runs constantly from the first launch, and there's only ever one Now Playing popup.

## Not in this version

- No settings carried over from 1.1.x. Everyone starts fresh.
- No NeoForge build.
- No title-screen button for the music screen. Open it in a world with M or from the pause menu.
- No per-song volume. No playlists beyond Basic's list and Custom's tags.
- No server-side features.

## UI overhaul — proposed phases, 2026-11-07

### Observed starting point

- `Theme.button` shares basic styling, but `MusicScreen` and `BasicPanel` each
  build settings and transport separately. Vanilla offers Pause/Resume; Basic's
  corresponding button currently stops playback. This is a behavior difference,
  not just a mismatched icon.
- `SongToast` replaces Basic notifications only; Vanilla delegates to Minecraft.
  Its long song line is clipped without scrolling.
- `CassetteArtwork` is already shared by pause, settings and browser tapes. It
  accepts one combined status/title and draws static crossed reels. `Track`
  currently stores one display component, not separate title and artist fields.

### Phase 1 — shared controls and notification behavior

- Retain the cassette identity, orange/blue/dark palette, current screen placement,
  Basic browser collapse/search/selection state, and Music-only volume control.
- Give shared settings and transport one UI owner used by both mode panels.
  `Theme` owns dimensions, spacing and colors; panels own placement and
  mode-specific additions. Remove superseded duplicate builders as they migrate.
- Use the same drawn Play, Pause, Resume and Next symbols, hit areas, tooltips,
  focus, hover and disabled states everywhere. Do not rely on `>` for both Play
  and Next or `[]` as a stop icon. Previous and Shuffle remain Basic-only.
- Recommended behavior: both modes use Play/Pause/Resume in the primary slot;
  keep Basic Stop as a separate, explicitly labeled action so held Stop remains
  available. Reuse the director's existing channel pause/resume rather than adding
  a player or UI-owned playback state. This proposal supersedes Basic's primary
  Play/Stop slot only when this phase is authorized.
- Use one replacement Now Playing toast in every implemented mode, respecting
  Minecraft's Music Toast option and notification duration multiplier. Update the
  same notification on a track change; suppress the vanilla duplicate in Vanilla
  too. This supersedes the earlier Vanilla-native-toast contract on approval.
- Check: packaged client exercises matching transport actions in Vanilla/Basic,
  separate Basic Stop, exactly one toast per transition and Music Toast Never.
  Do not alter Records or effects while handling background music controls.

### Phase 2 — readable cassette labels and overflow

- Show song title on the top line and artist underneath on every cassette: pause
  menu, both settings players and browser rows. Title is bright; artist is quieter
  but readable. Keep status such as Paused or Muted separate from song metadata.
- Introduce structured display metadata at the existing naming/library boundary;
  expose the current resolved track's metadata through the director. Consumers
  must not split translated status strings. Prefer explicit metadata; recognize
  the existing artist/title convention only in that naming owner. Preserve names
  containing hyphens, translation components and modded fallback titles. If the
  artist is genuinely unknown, leave that line empty rather than inventing one.
- Reserve two real font-height lines plus padding. Increase the compact pause
  housing only as needed, retaining top-center placement and avoiding vanilla
  pause controls. Do not shrink text to force it between the reels. At narrow
  widths, shrink/reposition the artwork before sacrificing readable label space.
- Share one clipped overflow renderer with the toast: fitting lines remain still;
  overflowing lines wait 1 second, move at a steady proposed 20 GUI pixels/second,
  pause 1 second at the far end, then reverse without a wrap jump. Title and artist
  overflow independently. Reset only when that line's content changes, not each
  frame. Toast starts showing the title immediately rather than losing space to
  a long heading. Full metadata remains available through tooltips/narration.
- Keep title/artist/file-ID search and existing ranking, ticks and browser position.
- Check: short/long title and artist, unknown artist, hyphenated and modded names,
  and a changed track. Inspect pause/settings/browser/toast at normal size and
  320×240; neither text nor scrolling may cross its label clip or cover a reel.

### Phase 3 — playback-driven reels and bounded visual polish

- Empty-player artwork (OG Moo-cow's addition): when nothing is playing, the
  pause-menu and settings now-playing area shows an empty player outline with
  no cassette inside. Its only interior details are two red spindle rods, exactly
  the same size and at the same positions as the existing reel-center dots at
  that artwork scale. Do not leave the old cassette body, label or reels visible.
  Paused music retains its loaded cassette with frozen reels; muted playback
  retains its loaded cassette too. Stop or no current track shows the empty player.
- Selection-list cassettes have open spindle holes where those reel-center dots
  currently sit, not red filled centers. The red rods belong to the player;
  cassette holes and player rods share geometry so their centers and sizes align.
  Preserve the holes while animating reel spokes, including the playing list row.
- Check the empty and loaded artwork at compact pause and full settings sizes,
  plus list-row holes at normal and narrow widths. Verify Stop/silence removes
  the cassette, Pause keeps it, and the red rods match the spindle-hole geometry.
- Animate reels inside `CassetteArtwork`, shared by compact and large tapes.
  Proposed speed is one revolution every 2 seconds using elapsed render time,
  not frame count. Draw visibly rotating spokes; keep the cassette shell still.
- The existing director owns the actual playing identity and transport state.
  UI observes them: the current tape turns while its channel advances, freezes
  while paused, and stops on silence or held Stop. Muting does not freeze an
  advancing channel. Jukebox-induced silence stops the old tape's motion once
  its channel stops; a fade that is still playing may continue turning.
- In the browser, animate only the row matching the actual resolved playing track,
  never all selected tracks. Match resolved identity, not the displayed name.
  Reuse that state on pause and settings tapes; no widget-owned playback timers
  or second state map. Visual animation timing belongs to the artwork owner.
- Normalize uneven spacing, alignment, cassette borders, control groups and
  selected/playing/hover/focus distinctions across the existing UI. Preserve
  interaction behavior and search/collapse/resize state. Future Custom/import
  screens must reuse these shared components; this does not authorize building
  those features now.
- Check: observe changing reel positions over time, freeze/resume, Stop/silence,
  mute, Next, and the matching versus nonmatching browser row. Inspect the
  affected surfaces at normal size, 320×240 and GUI scales 2/4 for overlap,
  clipping and uneven placement. Use the existing packaged-client harness for
  focused checks; build the jar under `runtime/whostoppedthemusic`.

Each authorized phase gets its scoped build/check and commit; no PR or release.
Record actual evidence here and distinguish automated checks from OG Moo-cow's
visual/listening acceptance. Do not run full suites for these ordinary phases.

## Release

- Version: 1.2.0 · Modrinth channel: release · Also ships to: Modrinth only (client mod)
- Changelog, in player words:
  - Rebuilt from scratch for Minecraft 26.3.
  - New Music screen (press M) with three modes: Vanilla, Basic and Custom.
  - Basic: pick any songs for one playlist that plays everywhere, with play, stop, skip, previous and shuffle.
  - Custom: choose which songs play in each biome and situation, and how often each one plays.
  - Add your own MP3 and OGG files: drop them in the `music` folder or drag them onto the Add Songs screen.
  - Music discs can be part of your music.
  - Music Frequency starts on Constant, and you can change it right in the Music screen.
  - Optional: background music pauses while a jukebox plays near you.
  - Only one Now Playing popup, in PyreHaven style.
